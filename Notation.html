1 Table of notation

Notation Quantity Dimensions
\(S(t)\), \(S_t\) Number of susceptible individuals at time \(t\)
\(E(t)\) Number of exposed individuals at time \(t\)
\(I(t)\), \(I_t\) Number of infectious individuals at time \(t\)
\(R(t)\) Number of recovered individuals at time \(t\)
\(N\) Population size
\(\R_0\) Basic reproduction number
\(\Reff\), \(\R_t\) Effective or time-varying reproduction number at time \(t\)
\(\R_v\) Reproduction number with vaccination
\(\lambda(t)\) Force of infection at time \(t\) \(T^{-1}\)
\(\inc(t)\) Incidence rate at time \(t\) \(T^{-1}\)
\(\inc_t\) Daily incidence on day \(t\)
\(\beta\) Transmission rate \(T^{-1}\)
\(\alpha\) Transition rate from exposed to infectious state \(T^{-1}\)
\(\gamma\) Recovery rate \(T^{-1}\)
\(w\) Immunity waning rate \(T^{-1}\)
\(\mu\) Mortality rate \(T^{-1}\)
\(r\) Epidemic growth rate \(T^{-1}\)
\(z\) Final epidemic size
\(p_v\) Proportion vaccinated
\(v_I\) Vaccine effectiveness against infection
\(v_T\) Vaccine effectiveness against transmission given infection
\(v_S\) Vaccine effectiveness against severe disease given infection
\(X_i\) Number of offspring form individual \(i\)
\(Z_t\) Number of new infections at time \(t\) or in generation \(t\)
\(G_X(.)\) Probability generating function for \(X\)
\(q\) Probability of extinction
\(a\) Age \(T\)
\(\tau\) Age of infection \(T\)
\(f(\tau)\) Average infectiousness at age-of-infection \(\tau\) \(T^{-1}\)
\(g(\tau)\) Generation interval probability density at age-of-infection \(\tau\) \(T^{-1}\)
\(g_\tau\) Generation interval probability mass function at age-of-infection \(\tau\)
\(K_{ij}\) Next generation matrix (expected number of infections of type \(i\) caused by an individual of type \(j\) in fully susceptible population)
\(C_{ij}\) Contact matrix (expected number of contracts per unit time of type \(i\) for an individual of type \(j\) \(T^{-1}\)
\(E(.)\), \(\langle . \rangle\) Expected value
\(\mathrm{Var}(.)\) Variance
